faqs:
  - question: What do you need to know about my machine?
    answer: Its weight, its loaded dimensions — especially height — and whether it moves under its own power. The make and model is usually enough for us to look the rest up.
  - question: Who is responsible for loading and securing?
    answer: Securing the load on the trailer is ours. Lifting or driving the machine on is usually arranged at the loading point, and we agree who does what before the vehicle arrives — it is worth settling that in advance rather than on the day.

Moving a machine is not the same as moving freight. The machine decides the trailer, the route and sometimes the time of day, and getting that wrong is expensive.

The photographs on this page are all our own vehicles on real jobs.
