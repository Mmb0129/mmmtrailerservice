A 40 ft high-bed flatbed is the vehicle most industrial loads travel on. The deck is flat, open on all sides, and sits at normal chassis height — so anything a crane or a forklift can place on it is a candidate.

It is the trailer we use most, and the one most of the photographs on this site show.
